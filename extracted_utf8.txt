About Us - Ceylon Petroleum Corporation Sapugaskanda Refinery Training Center
Established in 1967 during the construction of the Sapugaskanda Refinery, the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) Sapugaskanda Refinery Training Center has been a cornerstone of technical excellence in Sri LankaÆs petroleum industry. Founded to train skilled professionals for the safe and reliable commissioning and operation of the refinery, the center has evolved into a premier institution for developing highly competent technicians for both local and global energy sectors.
Since its inception, the Training Center has maintained continuous training cycles, beginning with the Shell commissioning team in 1969. In 1978, it partnered with the National Apprentice Board (now the National Apprentice and Industrial Training Authority, NAITA) to offer a rigorous four-year training program, awarding craftsmen certificates to successful trainees. Over the decades, the center has trained numerous groups of engineering professionals, many of whom have secured opportunities in the international petroleum industry.
Adapting to global standards, the Training Center embraced the National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) framework introduced in Sri Lanka in 2005, followed by the Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework (SLQF) in 2015. Recognizing the need for internationally accredited qualifications, the center collaborated with NAITA and the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC) to develop and validate NVQ Level 5 curricula for eight critical technical trades: Refinery Operation Technician, Refinery Engineering Technician (Mechanical, Electrical, Instrument), Refinery Laboratory Technician, NDT Technician, 6G Welder, and High Pressure Industrial Boiler Operator. These curricula, endorsed by TVEC as national standards, are designed to meet the demands of both local and international petroleum, oil, gas, and energy sectors.
With a legacy of over five decades, the CPC Sapugaskanda Refinery Training Center remains committed to fostering technical expertise, ensuring safe and efficient refinery operations, and empowering skilled professionals to excel in the global energy industry.

Foreword
The Sapugaskanda Refinery, established in 1969, stands as Sri Lanka's single largest oil refinery, fulfilling approximately 40% of the nation's petroleum-related product demands. For over five decades, the Refinery Training Center has been the cornerstone of technical workforce development for the petroleum industry in Sri Lanka.
This brochure presents the newly developed National Competency Standards (NCS) and Competency-Based Training (CBT) Curricula for eight specialized technician trades, developed in collaboration with the National Apprentice & Industrial Training Authority (NAITA) and endorsed by the Tertiary & Vocational Education Commission (TVEC). These qualifications, offered at NVQ Level 5, represent a landmark achievement in aligning Sri Lanka's refinery training with national and international vocational qualification frameworks.
The curricula are designed to produce world-class, multitasking technicians capable of meeting the demands of both the domestic petroleum sector and the international oil and gas industry.

1. About the Refinery Training Center
A Legacy of Excellence Since 1967
The Refinery Training Center was established during the construction period of the Sapugaskanda Refinery in 1967. Its primary mandate was to train the required employees to commission the refinery and maintain continuous training cycles, which commenced in 1969 with the Shell commissioning team.
Evolution of Training
1978: Recruitment of technical trainees through the National Apprentice Board (NAB) began. Trainees who successfully completed four years of training in various technical sections were issued craftsman certificates by the National Apprentice Board (now NAITA).
1990s: The international qualification framework (IQF) was introduced by developed countries, providing access to qualifications and facilitating movement between education, training, and the labor market.
2005: Sri Lanka introduced the National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) frameworkùa competency-based system with seven levels focusing on practical skills and knowledge for specific employment positions.
2015: The Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework (SLQF) was introduced, integrating all national and international qualification frameworks for verification purposes.
2016: Most Middle Eastern and European petroleum and petrochemical industries upgraded their qualification requirements, making internationally accepted certificates essential for employment.
A New Chapter
The Refinery Training Center has now developed and validated National Competency Standards and Curricula for eight technical trades, endorsed by TVEC as national documents. These curricula enable
trainees to train under new National Competency Standards (NCS) and receive internationally recognized NVQ Level 5 qualifications.
2. The National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) Framework
What is NVQ?
The National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) Framework is a seven-level qualification system that serves as Sri Lanka's national benchmark for quality-assured technical and vocational education and training (TVET). It provides nationally and internationally recognized certification for individuals seeking employment and professional pursuits.
NVQ Level 5 û Advanced Technical & Supervisory Skills
NVQ Level 5 qualifications focus on advanced technical and supervisory competencies, enabling individuals to oversee operations, make strategic decisions, and drive organizational growth. At this level, candidates demonstrate:
Advanced practical skills in specialized technical areas
Supervisory and management capabilities
Ability to work independently and make informed decisions
Comprehensive understanding of industry standards and safety protocols
Integration with SLQF
The NVQ Framework integrates with the Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework (SLQF), allowing for flexible pathways between vocational and academic qualifications. NVQ certifications are respected by employers and offer pathways to further education, including potential credit transfers into the SLQF academic framework.

A Refinery Operation Technician carries out refinery operations in the oil and gas industry according to industrial standards while ensuring occupational health and safety guidelines are followed.
Key Responsibilities

Check and ensure standby equipment for emergency requirements

Replace or adjust burners in process heaters

Perform recommissioning of equipment after maintenance

A Refinery Engineering Technician (Instrument) carries out installation, commissioning, and maintenance (preventive, corrective, and planned shutdown) on field and control center instrumentation in a petroleum refinery according to standard installation/maintenance practices and safety regulations to ensure smooth, safe, and efficient operation.

Install, commission, and maintain steam, boilers, turbo generators, diesel generators, water systems instruments and control systems

Maintain and repair Tank Gauging Systems (TGS) and Flow Metering Systems
Maintain and repair VHF communication equipment, PABX systems, and fire alarm systems
Fabricate control panels, instrument mounting structures, and manifolds
Maintain PLC/DCS based and hardwired Emergency Shutdown (ESD) systems
Maintain and calibrate analytical instruments in the refinery laboratory and online analyzers

8. Other Technician Trades
Additional NVQ Level 5 Programs
5. Refinery Laboratory Analyst
Specialized training in oil, gas, water, and product analysis. Trainees gain expertise in laboratory analytical instruments and quality control procedures essential for refinery operations.
6. Industrial Boiler Operator (High Pressure)
Multitasking trade covering power plant operations, water treatment, and steam systems. Trainees are qualified to operate and maintain high-pressure boilers and associated systems.
7. Oil and Gas Plant Inspection Technology
Multitasking trade encompassing Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), Risk-Based Inspection (RBI), and plant integrity management. Essential for ensuring the safe and reliable operation of refinery assets.
8. 6-G Industrial Welder
Multitasking trade including welder fabricator, combo welding, and NDT. Trainees achieve the internationally recognized 6-G welding certification, enabling them to work on critical refinery piping and structural components.
9. Training & Recruitment Process
Entry Requirements
Applications are invited through separate paper advertisements for each trade. Candidates must meet the following criteria:
Educational Qualifications:
Minimum of six subject passes in one sitting in GCE (O/L) examination, including credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil Language, Mathematics, and Science
Minimum of three subject passes in Science stream in one sitting at GCE (A/L) Examination
Age Limit:
Not more than 24 years as at the closing date of the application
Written Test:
Must pass a written test conducted for the respective trade
Training Program Structure
The training program is a comprehensiveá4-year full-timeáprogram:
Stage
Description
1. Application & Selection
Advertisement, written test, and interview
2. Institutional Training
Theoretical and practical sessions with mainly in-house resources, with NAITA monitoring and regular assessments
3. On-the-Job Training
Focused training in each discipline, attached to respective departments of the refinery or other locations for practical experience
4. Final Assessment
Final examinations by NAITA and the Refinery Training Centre with viva and practical tests
5. Certification & Absorption
Issuance of NVQ Level 5 certificate and final absorption to CPC permanent cadre
10. Career Pathways & Opportunities
National Opportunities
Upon successful completion of the program and absorption into the CPC permanent cadre, technicians can progress through various career paths, including senior technician positions, supervisory roles, and management positions such as Superintendent.
International Opportunities
Trainees not absorbed by the refinery will have opportunities in overseas oil and gas industries and local industries, as the new curricula are multitasking and internationally aligned. With NVQ Level 5 certification, technicians can apply for highly paid foreign jobs in the oil and gas sector.
Strategic Importance
Supporting Sri Lanka's Energy Hub Vision
The development of a skilled, qualified workforce in the oil and gas sector supports the government's policy to make Sri Lanka an energy hub. Recent initiatives, including the Memorandum of Understanding signed in April 2025 between Sri Lanka, India, and the UAE to develop Trincomalee as a regional energy hub, underscore the growing demand for qualified petroleum industry professionals.
Leveraging National Assets
The Sapugaskanda RefineryùSri Lanka's one and only refineryùserves as a unique training bay for the development of skilled workforce for both local and international markets.
Key Benefits of NVQ Level 5 Certification
